# Product Overview

Colectio Article Agent is an AI-powered system designed to generate, process, and manage articles for the Colectio platform. The agent handles content creation, editing, and optimization workflows to support automated article generation and content management.

## Key Features
- Automated article generation
- Content processing and optimization
- Integration with Colectio platform
- AI-driven content workflows

## Target Use Cases
- Content creation automation
- Article processing pipelines
- Content quality enhancement
- Scalable content management
